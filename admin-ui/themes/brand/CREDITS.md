# Where the logo pictures come from

## salmon.png: the logo

A live coho salmon (*Oncorhynchus kisutch*), cut out of the photo
"Coho salmon" by NOAA Fisheries, on the coho salmon page of
<https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/species/coho-salmon>.

NOAA's website policy says images created by NOAA are not subject to
copyright in the United States and may be used without permission, and
asks for "NOAA" to be credited as the source. Photo: NOAA Fisheries. Its
use here does not mean that NOAA endorses LosOS.

The cut-out is the photo with the river bed removed, turned 35 degrees so
the fish rises from left to right, and scaled into a 512 px square. Its
colours are left as photographed. `salmon-128.png` is the same picture at
128 px. `losos-cloud.svg` and `losos-git.svg` embed `salmon.png`.

## plate.png: the Halloween logo

A plate of salmon, cut out of a photo taken by the project's author, who
owns it. The admin pages and the handbook show it on 31 October only.
`plate-128.png` is the same picture at 128 px.
